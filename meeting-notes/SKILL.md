---
name: Meeting Notes Formatter
description: Format raw meeting notes into clean, structured summaries
---

## Instructions
When invoked, take raw meeting notes and:
1. Identify key decisions
2. Extract action items with owners
3. Summarize discussion points
4. Produce a clean, professional output

## Example
Input:
- Discussed Q1 roadmap
- Alice to draft proposal
- Bob to review budget

Output:
### Summary
We reviewed the Q1 roadmap...

### Action Items
- Alice — Draft proposal
- Bob — Review budget
